# LifeTrack privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

LifeTrack is a personal tracker for calories, activity, habits, a daily routine, screen time and
alarms. This policy explains what it reads and what happens to it.

## The short version

Everything LifeTrack records stays on your phone. LifeTrack has no account, no server, no
analytics, no ads and no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. The
developer never sees it.

## What LifeTrack reads, and why

| Data | Where it comes from | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Meals and foods you log, your profile (height, weight, age, activity level) | Typed in by you | Calorie, protein and fibre goals and history |
| Steps, distance, exercise sessions and their active calories, heart rate, sleep | Health Connect, only the types you allow | The Activity tab, calories burned and the routine's "walk" check |
| Steps from the phone's own step counter | Physical activity permission | Steps when Health Connect isn't used |
| How long each app you add to the Screen time list is open | Usage access | Daily screen-time totals and limits |
| Which app is in front, for apps with a limit | Usage access | Closing an app once its daily limit is used up |
| Habits, routine items, alarms and reminders | Typed in by you | Those features |

LifeTrack reads Health Connect data in the background (every 15 minutes) only if you allow it,
so steps stay current while the app is closed. It never writes to Health Connect.

## Where it is stored

In a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing is copied to a server.
If Android's own device backup is turned on, the app's data may be included in your Google
backup, under your Google account, like any other app's.

## Sharing

LifeTrack does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone.

## Keeping or deleting it

History older than six months is deleted automatically. Uninstalling LifeTrack, or clearing its
storage in Android Settings, deletes everything it stored. You can withdraw any permission at
any time in Android Settings or in Health Connect; the feature that needed it stops, the rest
keeps working.

## Children

LifeTrack is not directed at children under 13.
